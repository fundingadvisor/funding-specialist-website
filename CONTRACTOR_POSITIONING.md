## The Problem Companies Face

High-ticket programs and education companies often lose qualified prospects at the final stage of the sales process due to one simple barrier: **upfront cost**. Prospects love the program, believe in the value, and are ready to commit—but they don't have the cash available right now.

This creates a frustrating situation for both the company and the prospect. The company loses revenue from a qualified buyer, and the prospect misses out on a life-changing program because of temporary cash flow constraints.

---

## The Solution: Independent Funding Partnership

An independent funding specialist contractor bridges this gap by providing expert funding guidance to prospects during or immediately after the sales call. By offering accessible financing options (personal loans and credit cards), companies can convert qualified prospects who would otherwise walk away.

## How It Works

### The Process

**Step 1: Hot Transfer**
When your sales rep completes a sales call and the prospect is ready to discuss funding, they transfer the client directly to me via Slack in your designated funding channel. I receive the prospect's contact information and reach out immediately.

**Step 2: Initial Consultation**
I conduct a 20-30 minute consultation with the prospect to understand their financial situation. I guide them through pulling their own credit score and discuss their funding needs based on the program cost and any additional resources they might need.

**Step 3: Lender Matching**
Based on the prospect's credit profile and funding needs, I match them with the best available lender from my network of 5-6 vetted online lenders. I consider approval likelihood, interest rates, origination fees, and timeline to ensure the best outcome for the prospect.

**Step 4: Application Support**
I walk the prospect through the entire application process, answering questions and ensuring all information is accurate and complete. I handle the technical details so the prospect can focus on their decision.

**Step 5: Daily Follow-Up**
Once the application is submitted, I touch base with the prospect daily to monitor progress and address any questions from the lender. I provide your sales team with daily Slack updates so you're always informed of status.

**Step 6: Funding & Beyond**
When funds are received, I confirm with the prospect and notify your team. I can even assist with the product purchase if needed, ensuring a smooth transition from funding to onboarding.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much does this cost?**
A: Nothing upfront. I only get paid 4% of the completed product purchase amount when a prospect successfully gets funded and purchases your program. If they don't get funded, you don't pay anything.

**Q: What if a prospect doesn't qualify for funding?**
A: I work with all credit profiles, including bad credit, to find creative solutions. However, if a prospect truly doesn't qualify for any option, I'll let you know immediately so you can discuss alternative payment plans or options with them.

**Q: How long does the funding process take?**
A: Average timeline is 5 days for personal loans without complications. Credit cards take a minimum of 7 days. Some cases may take longer depending on lender requirements or credit complications.

**Q: Can you work with international clients?**
A: I specialize in US-based funding, which is significantly easier and faster. I can work with Canadian clients, but it's more complicated. Other countries are possible but not ideal.

**Q: Do you work exclusively with one company?**
A: No, I'm available to work with multiple companies simultaneously, depending on lead flow. This allows me to maintain consistent volume and availability.

**Q: What if a prospect wants to fund more than the program cost?**
A: That's common. Prospects often want extra funding for advertising, resources, or other needs. I help them determine the appropriate amount and apply accordingly. My commission is based on the amount actually used for the program purchase.

**Q: How do you stay compliant?**
A: I don't record any client calls to maintain compliance. I have no affiliations with specific lenders, allowing me to recommend objectively. I follow all applicable lending regulations and guidelines.

**Q: What communication tools do you use?**
A: I primarily use Slack for updates with your sales team and phone/text for direct client communication. I'm open to other communication methods that work for your company.
