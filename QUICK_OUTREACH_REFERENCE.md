## Things NOT to Do

❌ Don't be pushy or aggressive  
❌ Don't make promises you can't keep  
❌ Don't ignore follow-ups or miss deadlines  
❌ Don't send mass emails without personalization  
❌ Don't talk about yourself more than the prospect's needs  
❌ Don't forget to mention your website  
❌ Don't give up after one "no"  
❌ Don't work with prospects who don't have high-ticket programs
