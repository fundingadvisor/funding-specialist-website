# Comprehensive Website Review - Professional Upgrade Recommendations

## Overall Assessment
Your website is well-structured and professional. Here are specific areas that could be enhanced for better cohesion and professionalism:

---

## 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY ISSUES

### 1. **Inconsistent Language: "Hot Transfers" vs "Prospect Transfer"**
**Issue:** The website uses both terms inconsistently:
- Process section says: "Prospect Transfer" (Step 1)
- Services section says: "Hot transfers from your sales team via Slack"
- "How I Work" section says: "hot transfers"
- Partnership Requirements says: "Hot Transfers or Cold Transfers"

**Recommendation:** Standardize to ONE term throughout. Suggest "**Prospect Transfer**" (more professional) or keep "Hot Transfer" if you prefer the industry term. Update all instances for consistency.

**Locations to fix:**
- Services section (Expert Credit Evaluation card)
- How I Work paragraph
- Partnership Requirements section

---

### 2. **Outdated Language: "Only Get Paid"**
**Issue:** FAQ section still says: "I only get paid 4% of the completed product purchase..."

**Recommendation:** Update to match the professional tone of the Compensation Model section. Change to: "Compensation is 4% of the completed product purchase when a prospect successfully gets funded."

**Location:** FAQ - "How much does this cost?" question

---

### 3. **Inconsistent Terminology: "Hot Transfers" Definition**
**Issue:** The Partnership Requirements section says "Hot Transfers or Cold Transfers" but doesn't define what these mean. A company unfamiliar with the term might be confused.

**Recommendation:** Add brief clarification:
- **Hot Transfers:** Prospect handed off during/immediately after sales call
- **Cold Transfers:** Prospect information sent separately for follow-up

Or simplify to just "Prospect Transfers" and explain in the Process section.
