## Outreach Strategy: Prior Relationships vs. New Prospects

### For Tier 1 (Prior Relationships) - Companies You've Worked With

**Positioning:** You're now offering independent services with the advantage of process familiarity and proven track record.

**Key Message Template:**
"Hi [First Name],

I wanted to reach out because I've had the privilege of working with your team before, and I'm impressed with what you're building at [Company Name].

I'm now offering independent funding specialist services directly to companies like yours. Since I'm already familiar with your sales process and have successfully funded your clients, I thought it made sense to explore working together as an independent contractor.

Here's what I offer:
- Performance-based pricing (4% only when funded)
- Direct integration into your sales process
- Daily updates and support
- Proven track record with your client base

I'd love to discuss how we can scale your funding support. Would you be open to a quick 15-minute call this week?

Best,
Lynne"

**Outreach Channels:**
1. Direct email to decision-maker (highest priority)
2. LinkedIn connection + personalized message
3. Phone call (if you have direct contact)
4. Referral introduction (if mutual connection available)

**Timeline:** Start with Tier 1 immediately—these are warm leads with existing relationships.

### For Tiers 2-5 (New Prospects)

**Positioning:** You're a proven funding specialist with 5,000+ clients and are now seeking partnerships with high-ticket programs.

**Key Message Template:**
"Hi [First Name],

I'm Lynne Faulkner, an independent funding specialist. I've successfully funded 5,000+ clients for high-ticket coaching, e-commerce education, and digital marketing companies.

I work with programs like yours to solve a specific problem: qualified prospects who are ready to commit but don't have the cash available upfront. I help them access personal loans and credit card financing, removing that final price barrier.

Here's how it works:

- I handle all funding details and provide daily updates
- You only pay 4% of the completed purchase when funded
- Zero upfront cost, zero risk

I'd love to explore if this could be a fit for [Company Name]. Would you be open to a brief conversation?

Check out my website: https://funding-specialist-website.vercel.app

Best,
Lynne"

**Outreach Channels:**
1. Cold email (personalized for each company)
2. LinkedIn connection + follow-up message
3. Phone call (if contact found)

**Timeline:** Start with Tier 2 companies after Tier 1 outreach is underway.
