## 10. Key Talking Points to Remember

**Lead with the problem:** Most high-ticket programs lose qualified prospects at the final stage due to price.

**Emphasize performance-based pricing:** Compensation is earned at 4% when a prospect gets funded. Zero upfront cost, zero risk.

**Highlight your track record:** 5,000+ clients funded, 5+ years of experience, average 5-day timeline.

**Stress seamless integration:** Prospect transfers through preferred communication platform, daily updates, minimal training required.

**Mention flexibility:** You work with multiple companies simultaneously and can handle 20-30+ prospects per week.

**Address credit concerns:** You work with all credit profiles, including challenging ones, and have creative solutions.

**Be confident but humble:** You know your value, but you're not pushy. You're genuinely trying to help them solve a problem.

## 11. Best Practices for Outreach

**Personalization is key.** Always research the company and the person you're reaching out to. Reference something specific about their program or recent news. This shows you're not just sending mass emails.

**Lead with value, not features.** Don't start by talking about yourself. Start by identifying a problem they likely have (losing qualified prospects at the final stage) and positioning yourself as the solution.

**Be specific about results.** Instead of saying "I help companies increase conversions," say "I've successfully funded 5,000+ clients and work with all credit profiles."

**Make it easy to say yes.** Offer specific times for calls, provide your website link, and make it clear there's zero risk and zero upfront cost.

**Follow up consistently.** Most people won't respond to your first email. Plan a follow-up sequence and stick to it. But don't be annoying—be respectful of their time.

**Use social proof.** When possible, mention other companies you've worked with or ask for referrals from existing partners.

**Be authentic.** People want to work with people they like and trust. Be genuine, be helpful, and be honest about what you can and can't do.

**Track your results.** Keep track of which scripts and approaches get the best response rates. Adjust and optimize based on what works.

### Research Before Outreach

Before reaching out to any company, research the following:

1. **Their target audience:** Who are they selling to? What's their average student profile?
2. **Their program price:** What's the typical cost? This helps you understand if they're truly "high-ticket."
3. **Their sales process:** How do they sell? Do they have a sales team? This matters for integration.
4. **Recent news:** Have they recently raised funding, expanded, or launched new programs? Reference this in your outreach.
5. **Their leadership:** Who's the founder/CEO? Who runs sales? Who should you reach out to?
6. **Their social presence:** Follow them on LinkedIn, Instagram, or Twitter to stay updated and find connection opportunities.

## 13. Tracking & Optimization

### Create a Tracking Spreadsheet

Track the following for each outreach:

- Company name
- Contact name and title
- Date of initial contact
- Method of contact (email, LinkedIn, phone, etc.)
- Response received (yes/no/maybe)
- Date of response
- Next follow-up date
- Notes

### Metrics to Monitor

- **Response rate:** What percentage of your outreach gets a response?
- **Meeting rate:** What percentage of responses convert to meetings?
- **Partnership rate:** What percentage of meetings convert to actual partnerships?
- **Best channel:** Which outreach method gets the best response?
- **Best script:** Which script variation gets the most engagement?

### Optimization Tips

- Test different subject lines and measure open rates
- Try different times of day for emails and calls
- Experiment with different script approaches
- Track which industries respond best
- Identify which talking points resonate most
- Refine your approach based on data

## 14. Follow-Up Strategy

### The 5-Touch Follow-Up Sequence

**Touch 1 (Day 1):** Initial cold email  
**Touch 2 (Day 5):** First follow-up email  
**Touch 3 (Day 10):** Second follow-up email (last attempt)  
**Touch 4 (Day 15):** LinkedIn connection request + message  
**Touch 5 (Day 20):** Final email or phone call

If no response after 5 touches, move on to the next prospect. Don't waste time on people who aren't interested.

## Final Tips

1. **Be patient but persistent.** Building partnerships takes time. Don't expect immediate responses.
2. **Stay organized.** Keep track of all your outreach efforts so you don't miss follow-ups.
3. **Be authentic.** People can tell when you're being genuine. Be yourself.

6. **Keep improving.** Continuously refine your scripts and approach based on feedback and results.
