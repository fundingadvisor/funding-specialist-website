## 🎯 Website Positioning

This website positions you as an **independent 1099 contractor** available for B2B partnerships with high-ticket coaching, e-commerce education, and digital marketing companies. Unlike the previous version focused on general funding support, this version emphasizes:

- **Performance-based compensation** (4% of completed product purchase)
- **Seamless sales integration** (hot transfers via Slack)
- **Flexible availability** (3.5-hour daily shift + Calendly scheduling)
- **Ability to work with multiple companies simultaneously**
- **Proven track record** (100+ clients funded, 5+ years experience)

### 6. Process Section - "How It Works"

**6-Step Process:**

1. **Hot Transfer** - Sales rep hands off prospect via Slack
2. **Initial Consultation** - 20-30 minute call to understand situation
3. **Lender Matching** - Match with best lender based on credit profile
4. **Application Support** - Guide through complete application
5. **Daily Follow-Up** - Stay in touch and provide Slack updates
6. **Funding & Beyond** - Confirm funding and assist with purchase

**Key Metrics:**
- Average Funding Time: 5 Days (personal loans)
- Initial Consultation: 20-30 Minutes
- Funding Range: $1K - $60K (Average $10K)
- Capacity: 20-30+ Clients/Week

### 7. Compensation Section

**Pricing Model:** 4% of Completed Product Purchase

**Key Features:**
- ✓ No Upfront Fees
- ✓ Performance-Based (I succeed when prospects succeed)
- ✓ Calculated on Purchase Amount (not total funded)
- ✓ No Payment if Not Funded (zero risk to company)

**Example:**
- Prospect requests $15K in funding
- Uses $10K for program
- You pay 4% of $10K = $400

- **Headings:** Bold, larger sizes (1.75rem - 3rem)

### Layout Features
- **Responsive Grid Layouts** - Adapts from desktop to mobile
- **Card-Based Design** - Services, benefits, FAQs in cards
- **Gradient Backgrounds** - Hero section and highlights
- **Box Shadows** - Depth and visual hierarchy
- **Smooth Transitions** - Hover effects and animations
- **Mobile-First Approach** - Works perfectly on all devices

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Desktop:** 1200px+ (full layout)
- **Tablet:** 768px - 1199px (2-column grids)
- **Mobile:** 480px - 767px (1-column layout)
- **Small Mobile:** < 480px (optimized for small screens)
