# Tier 1 Outreach Scripts: Prior Relationships

## Overview

These scripts are specifically designed for companies you've previously worked with. The key advantage: they already know your work quality and understand your value. The positioning is different from cold outreach—you're offering independent services with the benefit of process familiarity and proven results.

## 3. Phone Script for Prior Relationships

### Opening (First 30 seconds)

"Hi [First Name], this is Lynne Faulkner. I hope you're doing well! I'm calling because I've really enjoyed working with your team at [Company Name], and I wanted to explore a new opportunity with you. Do you have about 15 minutes to chat?"

---

### If They Say "Sure, what's this about?"

"Perfect. So I'm now offering independent funding specialist services, and I thought it made sense to reach out to companies I've already worked with. Since I'm familiar with your sales process and have successfully funded your clients, I believe I could provide even better support as an independent contractor.

The model is still performance-based—4% commission only when a prospect gets funded. But now I can offer more flexibility and direct integration into your sales process. I'd love to discuss how we could work together to scale your funding support."

---

### If They Ask "What's Different from Before?"

"Great question. The main difference is that now I'm operating as an independent contractor, which means:

1. **Better for you:** No team overhead or coordination required. You work directly with me.
2. **More flexible:** I can tailor my availability and approach to your specific needs.
3. **Same proven results:** You already know the quality of my work. I've successfully funded your clients.
4. **Better pricing:** Performance-based model means compensation is earned only when a prospect gets funded.

Essentially, you get the same quality of service you've experienced before, but with more flexibility and direct support."

---

### If They Ask "How Much Does This Cost?"

"It's completely performance-based. My compensation is 4% of the completed product purchase when a prospect successfully gets funded. If they don't get funded, you don't pay anything. So there's zero risk and zero upfront cost to try this out."

---

### If They Ask "Can You Handle Our Volume?"

"Absolutely. I can handle 20-30+ prospects per week, and I'm familiar with your typical prospect profile and funding needs. I can also adjust my availability based on your peak sales times."

---

### If They Ask "How Do We Get Started?"

"Perfect. Here's what I suggest: Let's schedule a brief call where I can walk you through exactly how the process would work, answer any questions, and we can see if this is a fit. I'm available [mention your available times]. Also, check out my website at https://funding-specialist-website.vercel.app—it has all the details about my process and pricing.

Does [specific time] work for you?"

---

### Closing

"Thanks so much for your time. I really think this could be a great fit for [Company Name], and I'm excited about the possibility of working together again. Let's connect soon and explore this further."

## 7. Objection Handling for Prior Relationships

### Objection: "We're happy with our current funding solution."

**Response:** "That's great to hear! I'm not asking you to replace anything—I'm offering to enhance what you're already doing. Since I'm familiar with your process and have successfully funded your clients, I could help you scale your conversions even further. Would you be open to a quick conversation to explore how this might work?"

---

### Objection: "We don't have budget for this right now."

**Response:** "I completely understand. The good news is that it's completely performance-based—I only get paid 4% when a prospect gets funded. So there's no upfront cost or budget required. You literally only pay if it works. Would you be open to exploring this with zero financial risk?"

---

### Objection: "We're not looking to change our funding process."

**Response:** "I hear you. I'm not asking you to change your process—I'm offering to integrate directly into what you're already doing. Think of me as an additional resource that scales your current approach. Since I'm already familiar with your process, it would be a smooth integration. Would you be open to a brief conversation?"

---

### Objection: "We'll think about it and get back to you."

**Response:** "Perfect. I appreciate that. Just so you know, I'm actively building partnerships right now, so I'd love to move quickly if this is a fit. Would you be open to a brief call next week so we can explore this together? That way you can make a more informed decision."

## Key Takeaways for Tier 1 Outreach

1. **Lead with familiarity:** Emphasize that you already know their process
2. **Reference past success:** Mention specific success with their clients
3. **Highlight benefits:** Better terms, more flexibility, proven results
4. **Reduce risk:** Emphasize performance-based model and zero upfront cost
5. **Move quickly:** These are warm leads—prioritize them
6. **Personalize:** Customize each message to their specific program
7. **Follow up:** Use the sequence provided to stay top-of-mind
8. **Be confident:** You've already proven your value to them
