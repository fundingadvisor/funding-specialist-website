# High-Ticket Coaching & Education Companies - Prospect List

## Research Summary
Identified high-ticket coaching and education companies that offer premium programs and would benefit from funding support for their clients.

## Key Prospect Companies

### 1. Strategic Coach
- **Focus:** Business coaching for entrepreneurs
- **Target Market:** Entrepreneurs with $200K+ annual income, 3+ years in business
- **Program Type:** Quarterly workshops, 1:1 coaching, accountability
- **Website:** https://www.strategiccoach.com/
- **Outreach Angle:** Help their members access financing for business expansion

### 2. Mastermind - High Ticket
- **Focus:** 1-on-1 mentorship from successful entrepreneurs
- **Target Market:** Business owners wanting to scale
- **Website:** https://mastermind.highticket.io/

### 3. New World Sales

## Outreach Strategy

1. **Direct B2B Partnerships:** Partner with coaching companies to offer financing as a value-add to their members
2. **Co-Marketing:** Create joint marketing materials highlighting funding solutions
3. **Referral Programs:** Establish referral agreements for client introductions
4. **Webinar/Workshop:** Host joint webinars on "Financing Your Business Growth"
5. **Case Studies:** Develop case studies showing successful client financing stories
