These email templates are crafted specifically for Lynne Faulkner's outreach to high-ticket coaching and education companies. Each version emphasizes her unique value proposition: helping prospects access the best possible funding terms so they can confidently say "yes" to the program.

- Focus on client benefit, not just transaction
- Warm, consultative advisory tone
- Professional yet approachable

## Key Differentiators in These Emails

**1. Client-Centric Focus**
The emails emphasize finding clients the *best* terms, not just any terms. This positions you as genuinely invested in their success.

**2. End-to-End Expertise**
Both versions reference your understanding of the entire business—from funding through fulfillment. This is a powerful differentiator.

**3. Warm & Advisory Tone**
The language is professional but approachable. You're positioned as a trusted advisor, not a salesperson.

**4. Performance-Based Confidence**
The 4% commission structure is presented as a sign of confidence in your ability to deliver results.

**5. Zero Risk Positioning**
Emphasizing "you only pay when it works" removes objections before they arise.

**6. Professional Language**
Uses "compensation" and "commission" instead of "I get paid"—maintaining professional tone throughout.

## Tips for Sending

1. **Personalize each email** - Add specific details about the company or person when possible
2. **Use their name** - Always address them by first name if possible
3. **Keep it conversational** - These emails should feel like they're from a person, not a template
4. **Follow up** - If no response in 5-7 days, send a brief follow-up
5. **Include your website link** - Direct them to your professional landing page for credibility

## A/B Testing Recommendation

Consider sending:
- Version 1 (longer, more detailed) to decision-makers who appreciate thorough information
- Version 2 (concise) to busy executives who prefer brevity

Track which version gets better response rates and adjust accordingly.
